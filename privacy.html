Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

gmail-organizer is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail accounts.

  • What it accesses: message headers (sender, subject, list-unsubscribe header) and labels, through the Gmail API scope gmail.modify.
  • What it does: applies labels and archives messages according to a local rules file.
  • Where data goes: nowhere. All processing happens on the owner's own computers. No mail data is stored on a server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or AI training.
  • Tokens: OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machines and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
